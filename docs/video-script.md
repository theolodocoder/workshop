# 30-second videos (two versions)

Record with Loom or the Mac screen recorder at 1280×800, browser zoom 110%, cursor highlight on. No music. You talk over it, in your own voice. These lines are a guide, not a script to read word for word.

## Version A: rescue and audit (for "finish, fix or launch my app" and audit jobs)

| Time | On screen | You say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:05 | The home page with its two doors | "This is a typical AI-built shop, and the same shop after I rescued it." |
| 0:05 to 0:12 | The before shop, My orders, click **Try to open Ben's order**. His order pops open | "Signed in as a stranger, I can open someone else's order. That's what happens with no database security." |
| 0:12 to 0:17 | The rescued shop, same button: **Blocked** | "Same click on the rescued shop: blocked." |
| 0:17 to 0:25 | The rescue report, scrolling the fix cards and the before/after numbers | "Nine fixes, each with its own commit and a test that proves it. Security, speed, accessibility and error handling." |
| 0:25 to 0:30 | `/check` with a URL pasted in, the result list showing | "Paste your own site in and see what I'd fix first. Then let's talk." |

## Version B: frontend (for React and Next.js frontend jobs)

| Time | On screen | You say |

## Where each goes

- **Upwork portfolio cover:** version A (it shows the most in the first 5 seconds).
